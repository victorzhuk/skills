# Evidence and decision patterns

Most skills need domain instructions and a checkable finish line, not generic reasoning prompts.

## Make decisions reviewable

Request a concise record of:

- the observed facts and their sources,
- the relevant alternatives,
- the selected action and its trade-off,
- the check that confirms it.

Do not request private chain-of-thought. XML tags do not create hidden channels, and a skill cannot promise that tagged text will be withheld from the user.

## Separate dependent work

Use an ordered procedure when later steps need earlier evidence. Give each step an observable output. Keep independent analysis parallel only when the caller or host authorizes delegation.

## Handle uncertainty

Distinguish confirmed, inferred, and unresolved claims. Explain the missing evidence when it affects a decision. Avoid confidence scores without a defined measurement.

## Recover without taking ownership

Preserve caller scope and prior approval. Report a blocked prerequisite to the owner instead of inventing a new lifecycle, mandatory menu, or permission gate.

Use a template only when downstream tools or reviewers require its fields. Otherwise, concise prose is enough.
