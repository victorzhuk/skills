# Research a market

## Collect evidence

1. Define the question, segment, and geography. Search category terms, alternatives, comparison sites, and relevant launch directories.
2. Read each vendor's pricing page. Record each tier’s price, currency, billing period, limits, and feature gates.
3. Check monthly and annual prices separately. Record any advertised annual discount. Record undisclosed prices as undisclosed.
4. Read vendor positioning. Attribute claims about features and outcomes to their source.
5. Check independent demand signals such as search trends, user complaints, and relevant hiring. A job posting alone cannot establish purchasing budget.

## Describe the customer hypothesis

Identify the buyer or user, company size, industry, problem, and current workaround.
Cite the evidence for each hypothesis. Identify any remaining assumptions.
An unsuccessful search establishes what you searched. It cannot establish absence across the market.

## Deliver the digest

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Question | The decision the research informs. |
| Answer | Two to five findings with source links. |
| Competitors | Name, positioning, reported price and terms, source, and access date. |
| Gaps | Missing evidence, search scope, and uncertain inferences. |
| Sources | Title, direct URL, and access date. |

Check every number against its source. Preserve original currencies and billing terms.
Keep recommendations about pricing or market entry for a separate strategy request.
